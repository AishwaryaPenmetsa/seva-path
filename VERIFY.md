## 4. Human Verification Checklist

To verify any link or policy in this codebase:
1. Ensure the URL resolves over HTTPS and the domain suffix matches official standards (`.gov.in`, `.nic.in`, `.ac.in` or established partner domain).
2. Check that the applicant guidelines reflect the most recent state or central notification.
3. SevaPath does not charge fees or request banking credentials; all transactions are routed directly to official departmental endpoints.
